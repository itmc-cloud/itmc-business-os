ITMC Bussines OS helps software and development service businesses quote, invoice, and get paid, without spreadsheets or a subscription.

FEATURES

- Client list: keep contact and billing details for everyone you work with.
- Service catalog: define your services and rates once (e.g. "Backend API development" per hour), then reuse them in every estimate instead of retyping prices.
- Cost estimates: build a line-item estimate for a client, pull items straight from your catalog, adjust quantity/tax/discount, and see the total update live.
- One-tap invoicing: convert any accepted estimate into an invoice with a generated invoice number and due date, or start an invoice from scratch.
- PDF export: share a clean, professional PDF of any estimate or invoice directly from the app.
- Status tracking: mark estimates as sent/accepted and invoices as sent/paid so you always know what's outstanding.

PRIVACY

All data stays on your device. There is no account, no login, and no data is sent to any server. Nothing is collected, tracked, or shared with third parties.
