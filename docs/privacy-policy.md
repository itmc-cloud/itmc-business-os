# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-08-15**

ITMC Bussines OS does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data, usage data, or analytics of any kind.

- **No account or login.** The app has no sign-in and no user accounts.
- **No server.** The app has no backend. It does not make network requests
  to any server operated by us or anyone else.
- **All data stays on your device.** Clients, service catalog entries,
  estimates, and invoices you create are stored only in a local database on
  your device (SQLite), and are never uploaded anywhere.
- **No third-party sharing.** Since nothing leaves your device, there is
  nothing to share with advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third
  party.
- **PDF export/share.** When you use the app's PDF export feature, the
  generated PDF is handed to Android's native share sheet, which is entirely
  under your control — you choose the destination (email, messaging app,
  save to file, etc.). The app itself does not transmit that PDF anywhere.

If this app ever adds a feature that changes any of the above (e.g. optional
cloud sync), this policy will be updated first, and that update will be
reflected in the app's changelog.
